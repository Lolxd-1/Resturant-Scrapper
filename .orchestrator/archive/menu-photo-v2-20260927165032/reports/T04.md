## Concerns
- **TC12's `>= 180` threshold is not achievable with correct, spec-exact parsing of the real `zcache/*order.html` fixtures.** Measured against all 185 real order pages: total items extracted = 20,459 (exactly matches `.orchestrator/recon/findings.md`'s independently recon-verified count, veg 13,837 / non-veg 6,232 / egg 390 — strong evidence the traversal itself is correct), but only 144 restaurants have >= 1 item with an http(s) image. The other 41 are real, verified data gaps, not parser bugs: 30 restaurants (mostly bars/breweries/hotel dine-in venues, e.g. `72 Bar Holiday Inn Sakinaka`, `Bira 91 Taproom`, `Big Bison`) have no `order`/`menuList` section anywhere in the page state at all (dine-in only, not a KeyError from bad parsing — confirmed by grepping the raw HTML for `menuList`/`categories`, both absent); 11 more (e.g. `Dorf Brewhouse`, `Skylit Club Lounge`) have a full order menu where every single item's `item_image_url` is `""` and `media` is `[]` (confirmed by dumping raw item JSON — genuinely no photos, not a fallback-logic gap; `media`-fallback fired 0 times across all 185 pages/20,459 items, so it's inert on this fixture set but kept per spec). I adjusted the test's assertion to `>= 140` (safely below the measured 144) and documented the measurement inline in the test rather than lowering it further or weakening the check to a trivial `> 0`. This is a data-shape fact, not an implementation gap — flagging for the orchestrator in case downstream tasks (T05 pool building, T10 crawl budget) assumed a higher photo-coverage rate from live pages.

## Discoveries
- Real Zomato restaurant pages commonly have **zero** delivery-menu items with photos (bars, breweries, hotel dine-in restaurants) — about 22% of a general city sample (41/185) had no imaged items at all, either because there's no embedded order menu at all or because none of their items were photographed. Downstream tasks that assume "most live-fetched restaurants contribute pool items" should budget for this.
- `item.get("media")` fallback for `item_image_url` never fired on any of the 185 real cached pages (0 of 7,992 no-`item_image_url` items had a usable `media[*].image.url`) — real pages simply omit both fields together when unphotographed. The fallback is still correct per spec/findings.md and costs nothing, just noting it's unexercised by real data.
- `state["pages"]["current"]["resId"]` is an `int` in real pages (not a string); `parse_menu` casts with `str()` before using it as the `state["pages"]["restaurant"]` dict key, as the brief specifies.
